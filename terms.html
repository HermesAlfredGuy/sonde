Terms

Last updated 12 September 2026

Sonde is a fictional product. These terms describe how a real pilot would be run, for the purposes of the design study.

The pilot

Six weeks, one asset, read only. No card is taken and no procurement process is required. Either side can stop at the end of any week.

Approval

Writes only happen through the gate, with a named approver from your organisation. Sonde cannot disable the gate, and cannot approve on your behalf.

Your data

You keep ownership of every row, query and log. On exit we hand over the audit trail and delete our copies within 30 days.

Availability

Pilots run with a named engineer and a four hour response window during business hours, Central Time.
