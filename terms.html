Terms

Last updated 12 September 2026

Sonde is a concept build, so these terms describe the pilot as it would run rather than a contract in force.

The pilot

Six weeks on one asset, connected to a replica. No card is taken and no procurement process is required. Either side can stop at the end of any week.

Approval

Writes happen only through the gate, with a named approver from your organisation. Sonde cannot disable the gate and cannot approve on your behalf.

Your data

You keep ownership of every row, query and log. On exit we hand over the audit trail and delete our copies inside 30 days.

After the pilot

Priced per asset, fixed for the first year. No seat count, no per query fee.
